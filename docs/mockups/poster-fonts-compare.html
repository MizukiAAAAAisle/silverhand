POSTER FONTS — SIDE BY SIDE

Comparison roster for forza6's --font-display. Same sample text and same colour palette in every card so weight, proportion, x-height and kerning are the only variables.

Four samples per card: a 64px headline (large display), a black metadata strip (date + counter, mimics our task / sidebar pills), a label-next-to-body row showing how the face sits next to Inter (body / chrome label secondary), and a **chrome label cascade** at the four real UI sizes (20 / 14 / 11 / 9 px, uppercase + 0.06em tracking — added per shiki feedback after Bebas in real app: headline-size sells, but most consumers live at 8-20px and that's where readability actually matters).

Cards 13-14 add the regular (non-display) weight of the two families that ship one: Oswald 500 (vs 700 in card #4) and Big Shoulders Display 400 (vs 800 in card #10). All other roster entries are single-weight by design — the cascade row is the only smaller-size proxy for them.
